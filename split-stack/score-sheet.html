SPLIT STACK — WHAT A SCORE LOOKS LIKE WHEN IT LANDS

The points from a call, on the pile it was called on: pops in a size too big, settles, rises a little, gone at a second. Real renderer, real card art, at the size it ships at. Every payout in the game is here — +1 for a placement, +3 for a suit, +4 for a split, −2 for a kill — each on a card chosen to be awkward for its colour.
